About CTC Freight Carriers

Leading the way in innovative logistics solutions since 2002

Our Story

Founded in 2002, CTC Freight Carriers has grown from a small regional player to one of India's leading logistics providers. Our journey has been marked by continuous innovation, unwavering commitment to quality, and a deep understanding of our customers' needs.

Today, we operate a modern fleet of over 500 vehicles, serving businesses across 28 states in India. Our temperature-controlled solutions and specialized services have made us a preferred partner for industries ranging from pharmaceuticals to fresh produce.

Message from Our Founder

Transportation of goods from manufacturing unit to retailers or from suppliers to retailers is an area of concern for all businesses. Established in the year 2002, at Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, we "C. T. C. Freight Carriers India Private Limited," have emerged as one of the leading solution providers in the field of Freight Transportation.

Our business operations are led by Mr. Arun Goyal, who brings extensive experience in this industry sector. His dedicated business approach and the support of experienced industry professionals ensures prompt delivery of goods in safe conditions as per defined time guidelines.

The support provided by our specialized fleet of trailers, trucks, and LCV's ensures safe handling of project transportation and ODC consignment. Our well-connected network of associates and branches across India enables us to successfully handle complete logistic service needs of our customers.

The high level of services delivered can be seen from the appreciation and repeat orders we receive from our clients. Furthermore, our competitive pricing adds to the contentment of our clients.
